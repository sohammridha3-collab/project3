🎯 Section A: Project Overview

Problem Statement

Digital communication channels (SMS, email, instant messages, and phone calls) are overwhelmed by sophisticated social-engineering attacks. Attackers weaponize urgency, fake authority, lottery lures, bank account locks, and impersonation to deceive everyday users into handing over sensitive credentials, one-time passwords (OTP), and financial funds. Traditional keyword filters and static blocklists fail against zero-day phrasing, subtle obfuscation, and multilingual voice-based scams.

Project Objectives

  • Machine Learning Ingestion: Classify digital communications using a trained dual-representation TF-IDF and Linear Support Vector Classifier (LinearSVC) pipeline.
  • Explainable AI & Threat Heuristics: Pair raw classification outputs with transparent, human-readable indicator warnings (urgency markers, suspicious URL patterns, payment requests) to empower user awareness.
  • Multilingual Voice Defense (CallScam): Transcribe and screen call recordings in English, Hindi, and Bengali using a synchronized speech-to-text and multilingual fraud heuristic pipeline.
  • Production-Grade Cybersecurity Architecture: Provide secure, user-isolated database persistence (SQLite/PostgreSQL), NIST-approved PBKDF2 password hashing, JWT authorization, and executive PDF audit reporting.

🧠 Section B: How the System Works

6-Stage Threat Detection Flow

Every incoming message travels through an end-to-end NLP screening pipeline executed inside our FastAPI server:

01 · Input Sanitization
Length Bounding & Normalization
Restricts input to 20,000 characters and structures emails into SUBJECT: ... BODY: ... matching training data.
02 · Dual TF-IDF Vectorizer
120,000 Sparse Features
FeatureUnion combining Word TF-IDF (1–2 n-grams, 80k features) and Character TF-IDF (3–5 n-grams, 40k features).
03 · LinearSVC Classification
Hyperplane Margin Evaluation
Computes geometric dot product z = w · x + b to classify on the optimal separating hyperplane.
04 · Platt Sigmoid Calibration
Calibrated Probability Score
Maps margin z into [0, 1] using σ(z) = 1 / (1 + e-z) for intuitive threat confidence gauges.
05 · Heuristic Rule Inspection
Explainable Attack Patterns
Flags urgency markers, suspicious URL shorteners, financial lures, and credential requests.
06 · Persistence & Audit PDF
Relational DB & ReportLab PDF
Saves user scan history with cascade isolation and generates downloadable PDF audit reports.

Training Dataset: CEAS 2008

The text classifier was trained and evaluated on the CEAS 2008 Email Spam/Ham Dataset, comprising 39,154 verified records (20,296 clean legitimate messages and 18,858 confirmed phishing/scam samples). Evaluated using an 80/20 train/test split.

📞 Section D: CallScam — Audio & Multilingual Voice Detection

Overview of CallScam Engine

CallScam extends threat screening into the voice and audio domain. Users upload recorded calls or capture voice samples directly in their browser. The system normalizes the audio, transcribes speech across English, Hindi, and Bengali, and screens the conversation for financial fraud, urgent threats, and impersonation cues.

Multilingual Speech-to-Text Pipeline

01 · Browser Audio Normalization
Web Audio API Resampling
Decodes MP3, M4A, WebM, and WAV in-browser to standard 16 kHz 16-bit PCM mono WAV before transmission.
02 · Multilingual Transcription
English, Hindi & Bengali
Transcribes speech with automatic language routing: en-US (English), hi-IN (हिन्दी), and bn-IN (বাংলা).
03 · Script & Language Detection
Unicode Script Verification
Identifies native Devanagari (\u0900-\u097F), Bengali (\u0980-\u09FF), and Latin scripts with code-switching tolerance.
04 · Cross-Lingual Threat Scoring
Native Heuristics + ML Hyperplane
Evaluates native language fraud keywords (e.g. बैंक खाता, ওটিপি, লটারি) combined with ML model decision margins.

Important Voice Recording Limitations & Safeguards

  • Microphone vs. Cellular Call Audio: Web browsers only have permission to capture the user's local microphone input. A browser cannot silently tap or record both sides of a cellular phone call due to operating-system security sandboxes. For cellular calls, users must use an authorized call recording app and upload the resulting audio file.
  • Explicit Legal Consent: Wiretapping and recording privacy regulations in many jurisdictions require two-party consent. ScamShield AI requires users to obtain consent before recording or analyzing conversations.
  • Speech Recognition Boundary Conditions: Background acoustic noise, heavy accents, and low bitrate telecom audio can degrade transcription accuracy. If transcription confidence is low, the system returns an Inconclusive verdict rather than an inaccurate false positive.

🔒 Section F: Privacy, Safety & Limitations

Zero-Trust Principle & Model Limitations

Cybersecurity engineering mandates full transparency regarding boundary conditions:

  • Negative Result != Guaranteed Safety: A "Clean / Legitimate" verdict indicates that the message or transcript does not match known threat patterns in the training distribution. It does not guarantee 100% safety. Attackers continually invent new pretexting methods.
  • No Verification of Caller Identity: The system analyzes linguistic content and threat indicators. It cannot verify whether an incoming phone number or email address has been spoofed at the carrier/protocol level.
  • Data Retention & Deletion Rights: Guest scans are processed in-memory and discarded. Authenticated users have full control to view or permanently purge individual scans or their entire history at any time. Uploaded audio files are stored in temporary memory during transcription and immediately cleaned up.

Universal Safe Messaging & Calling Guidelines

1. Never disclose OTPs: Legitimate bank representatives and support staff will never request your SMS OTP, PIN, or banking passwords over phone, SMS, or email.
2. Verify via Secondary Channel: If an urgent account suspension, tax audit, or legal arrest threat is received, hang up and call the organization's verified public hotline directly.
3. Do not install remote assistance apps: Never install apps like AnyDesk, TeamViewer, or QuickSupport on the instruction of an unverified caller.
